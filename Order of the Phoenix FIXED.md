# Order of the Phoenix FIXED: Outline

A planning outline for a fan-written alternate take on Book 5, continuing from *Goblet of Fire FIXED*. Plot points are summarized from memory, so check details against the book as needed.

**Key:** Unmarked points stay as they are in the book. **CHANGE** marks a plot edit. **NEW** marks an added beat.

## Where Book 4 left things

- **Harry and Hermione:** They became a couple privately after the lake, and Rita's article made it public. They survived their first fight and ended the year together, but after the graveyard Harry had started shutting her out.
- **Rita Skeeter:** Hermione caught her as a beetle and forbade her from writing for a year.
- **Ron and Élodie:** Élodie went home to France with Beauxbatons. She and Ron agreed to write over the summer.
- **Krum:** He has no romantic link to Hermione.

## Core changes

1. **Main romance:** Book 5 asks whether Harry and Hermione's relationship can survive Harry's angriest, most isolated year. The danger isn't another person. It's Harry pushing her away.
2. **Prefects:** Harry and Hermione are made Gryffindor prefects instead of Ron. Their shared duties give them time together, and Umbridge threatens to take Harry's badge away.
3. **Ron:** He doesn't want to be a prefect and is happy for Harry and Hermione. His arc is about his nerves as Keeper and winning the Quidditch Cup. Élodie appears only through letters, and she's part of why he's content.
4. **Occlumency:** Harry's link to Voldemort gives him a new fear: if Voldemort can see into his mind, he can see Hermione.

## Harry and Hermione's arc

1. **Silence (Part 1):** Her vague summer letters feel like a betrayal from his girlfriend, not just a friend.
2. **The blowup (Part 1):** His anger at Grimmauld Place hits her hardest, and they start the year on shaky ground.
3. **Prefects together (Part 2):** Their late-night rounds are their only time alone, but also make it harder for Harry to hide what's wrong.
4. **Partnership (Part 3):** Founding the DA together reminds them that they're best as a team.
5. **The threat (Part 4):** The snake attack and Occlumency make him afraid that being close to him puts her in danger.
6. **The interview (Part 5):** She hands him a way to fight back by arranging his interview with Rita Skeeter.
7. **His father (Part 6):** Snape's Worst Memory makes him question what kind of person and partner he is.
8. **The warning (Part 7):** She tells him the vision could be a trap. He goes anyway, and she nearly dies.
9. **The breakup (Part 8):** After Sirius's death and the prophecy, he ends the relationship "to protect her," and she tells him that's a stupid reason.

## Part 1: Summer and Grimmauld Place

- Dementors attack Harry and Dudley in Little Whinging. Harry drives them off with his Patronus, and Mrs. Figg reveals she's a Squib watching over him.
- The Ministry tries to expel Harry for underage magic. Dumbledore delays the decision until a hearing.
- All summer, Harry gets almost no news. Ron and Hermione's letters are vague, because Dumbledore has told them not to say anything.
- **CHANGE:** Hermione's letters now feel worse because she's his girlfriend. She writes carefully, misses him, and can't explain anything. Harry starts reading between the lines and assumes the worst.
- **NEW:** Ron's letters mention Élodie, who writes from France. Harry is jealous that Ron's long-distance relationship seems easier than his.
- The Advance Guard, including Moody, Lupin, and Tonks, takes Harry to Grimmauld Place, the Order's headquarters and Sirius's family home.
- Harry explodes at Ron and Hermione for keeping him in the dark.
- **CHANGE:** Most of his anger lands on Hermione. She takes it, then tells him that she argued with Dumbledore for him all summer. He's ashamed but too angry to apologize properly.
- **NEW:** That night, they make up awkwardly on the stairs. It's not quite fixed, but she tells him that she'll keep showing up even when he makes it hard.
- The house is full of Kreacher, the screaming portrait of Mrs. Black, and dark objects to clean out. Harry learns about the Order, Voldemort's search for a weapon, and the Ministry's refusal to believe him.
- Harry's hearing is moved to the full Wizengamot. Dumbledore and Mrs. Figg help clear him, but Dumbledore won't look at him.
- **NEW:** Hermione is waiting outside the courtroom with the Weasleys and is the first to hug him. Mr. Weasley tactfully finds something else to look at.
- **CHANGE:** Harry and Hermione are made Gryffindor prefects, not Ron and Hermione.
  - **Why Harry:** In this version, Dumbledore chooses Harry as a quiet message that he trusts him, even while he keeps his distance. Harry is too angry to see it that way and assumes it's a consolation prize.
  - **Ron:** He's openly relieved it isn't him. Prefect rounds would cut into his time writing to Élodie, and he enjoys teasing Harry and Hermione about their new patrols together.
  - **Mrs. Weasley:** She's delighted for Harry and Hermione and makes a huge fuss over them.
  - **Fred and George:** They're horrified that Harry has gone over to the side of authority and mock him all summer.

## Part 2: Back to Hogwarts and Umbridge

- Harry meets Luna Lovegood on the train and starts seeing thestrals.
- **CHANGE:** Harry and Hermione go to the prefects' carriage, where Malfoy is the Slytherin prefect, and Ron rides with Neville, Ginny, and Luna. Ron meets Luna first, so it becomes his friendship rather than Harry's at the start.
- **NEW:** Harry's first prefect rounds with Hermione are their first real time alone since Grimmauld Place. They patrol the corridors, catch Fred and George testing products on first-years, and quietly enjoy being a team again.
- **NEW:** The rounds also make it impossible for Harry to hide things from her. She's with him every evening, so she notices when he isn't sleeping.
- The Sorting Hat warns that the houses must unite.
- Dolores Umbridge, the Ministry's new Defense teacher, refuses to teach practical magic.
- Most students believe the Prophet over Harry. Seamus questions whether he's telling the truth.
- Harry speaks out in Umbridge's class and gets detention with a blood quill that cuts its words into his hand.
- **NEW:** Umbridge threatens to take away Harry's prefect badge if he keeps "spreading lies." It's a public test of whether he'll stay quiet, and he refuses.
- **CHANGE:** Harry hides the cuts from everyone, especially Hermione. She finds out anyway during their rounds and is furious that he didn't tell her. This begins the book's pattern: he hides pain to protect her, which hurts her more.
- **NEW:** She treats his hand with murtlap essence, which she researched herself. It's a quiet scene, and they both nearly say more.
- Educational Decrees give Umbridge more power. She becomes High Inquisitor and starts inspecting teachers.
- **NEW:** As a small setup for Book 6, Umbridge's office has started reading student owl post. Hermione notices first that the seals on her letters have been tampered with.

## Part 3: Dumbledore's Army

- Hermione proposes that Harry teach defense to students in secret.
- **CHANGE:** The idea comes from her faith in him, not just her concern about exams. She tells him he's the only person who can do it. He doesn't believe her, but he believes that she believes it.
- Students meet in the Hog's Head and sign Hermione's hidden jinxed list. Umbridge bans student groups.
- Dobby shows them the Room of Requirement, and Dumbledore's Army begins.
- **NEW:** Harry is a natural teacher, and Hermione watches him with obvious pride. Fred and George tease them relentlessly.
- **CHANGE:** Cho joins the DA as a background member, with no personal storyline with Harry.
- Hermione makes enchanted fake Galleons so the DA can communicate.
- Ron makes the Gryffindor Quidditch team as Keeper. The Slytherins mock him with the song "Weasley Is Our King."- **NEW:** Ron writes to Élodie, who replies with unhelpful but enthusiastic advice about French Quidditch tactics. It's a small comic beat.
- Malfoy provokes Harry and George after a match, and they're banned from Quidditch for life.
- **CHANGE:** Umbridge also strips Harry's prefect badge. It stays revoked until Dumbledore restores it at the end of the year.
- **NEW:** Hermione is angrier about the ban than Harry. She drafts a formal complaint to the Ministry, then burns it, knowing it would only reach Umbridge.
- Hagrid returns from his mission to the giants, injured and secretive.

## Part 4: The Snake and Christmas

- Harry dreams that he's a snake attacking Mr. Weasley at the Ministry. Dumbledore believes him, and Mr. Weasley is saved.
- Harry realizes he experienced the attack from inside the snake. He fears Voldemort is possessing him.
- **CHANGE:** His first fear is for Hermione: if Voldemort can get inside his head, he could find out what she means to Harry. He begins pulling away from her without explaining why.
- The Weasleys, Harry, and Hermione spend Christmas at Grimmauld Place. Sirius is happy to have them.
- **NEW:** Hermione corners Harry and makes him admit what he's afraid of. She tells him that pushing her away won't keep her safe, because she's Muggle-born and Harry Potter's best friend, so she's already a target. She'd rather face it with him.
- **NEW:** They spend Christmas Eve at Grimmauld Place together. It's their happiest moment in the book, with Sirius delighted to embarrass Harry.
- At St Mungo's, they meet Lockhart and Neville's parents.
- Dumbledore orders Harry to take Occlumency lessons with Snape.

## Part 5: Occlumency and Valentine's Day

- Snape forces his way into Harry's memories while Harry fails to block him.
- **CHANGE:** Snape sees memories of Hermione, including the lake and the Yule Ball. Harry is humiliated. It also makes him more afraid of what Voldemort could see.
- Ten Death Eaters escape from Azkaban, including Bellatrix Lestrange. The Ministry blames Sirius.
- **CHANGE:** The Valentine's date is cut, since Harry and Hermione already have plenty of time together on prefect rounds.
- **CHANGE:** On a Hogsmeade weekend, Hermione takes Harry straight to the Three Broomsticks, where she has secretly arranged a meeting with Rita Skeeter and Luna. Harry is wary of Rita at first, then realizes Hermione has given him a chance to tell the truth.
- Harry tells his story of Voldemort's return to Rita, and Luna's father publishes it in The Quibbler. Umbridge bans the magazine, making everyone read it.
- **CHANGE:** This is possible because Hermione has had leverage over Rita since Book 4.
- **NEW:** Students start believing Harry, and the letters he receives include some for Hermione, thanking her for standing by him.
- Umbridge sacks Trelawney, and Dumbledore hires Firenze.

## Part 6: Dumbledore Flees and Snape's Worst Memory

- Marietta, Cho's friend, betrays the DA to Umbridge.
- Dumbledore takes the blame for the DA and escapes. Umbridge becomes headmistress, and Filch gains new powers.
- Malfoy and the Inquisitorial Squad take points from Hermione and other students.
- During Occlumency, Harry uses Snape's Pensieve and sees one of Snape's worst memories: James and Sirius bullying Snape at school. Snape calls Lily "Mudblood" when she defends him.
- **CHANGE:** This hits Harry from both sides. His father was a bully, and Snape threw a slur at the girl who stood up for him. Harry is dating a Muggle-born girl who always stands up for him, and he wonders if he's more like either of them than he wants to be.
- **NEW:** Harry finally confides in Hermione about the memory instead of hiding it. She tells him that people aren't only their worst moments, but they are responsible for what they do next. This line matters again in Book 7.
- Harry talks to Sirius and Lupin through the fire in Umbridge's office. They admit that James was arrogant but grew up.
- Fred and George leave Hogwarts in spectacular style.
- Hagrid shows Harry and Hermione his giant half-brother, Grawp, in the Forbidden Forest.
- Ron becomes a star Keeper and wins Gryffindor the Quidditch Cup while Harry and Hermione are in the forest.
- **NEW:** Ron's moment is fully his. He writes to Élodie first, and Harry and Hermione are proud of him rather than feeling sidelined.

## Part 7: The Vision and the Ministry

- During the O.W.L.s, Umbridge's people try to arrest Hagrid. McGonagall is badly hurt defending him.
- In the History of Magic exam, Harry has a vision of Voldemort torturing Sirius in the Department of Mysteries.
- **CHANGE:** Hermione warns him it's probably a trap and calls out his "saving-people thing." It becomes a real argument between them: she's begging him to stop, while he can't bear to lose Sirius. She gives in when she sees he's going regardless, and she won't let him go alone.
- Umbridge catches them trying to contact Sirius through her fire. Hermione tricks Umbridge into the forest, where the centaurs carry Umbridge away.
- Harry, Hermione, Ron, Ginny, Neville, and Luna fly thestrals to the Ministry.
- In the Hall of Prophecy, Lucius Malfoy and the Death Eaters ambush them. Sirius was never there.
- The students fight their way through the Department of Mysteries.
- Dolohov hits Hermione with a silent curse that nearly kills her.
- **CHANGE:** Harry thinks she's dead. Neville finds her pulse. In this version, it's the most frightening moment Harry has ever experienced, and it confirms his fear that being close to him nearly killed her.
- The Order arrives. Bellatrix kills Sirius, who falls through the Veil.
- Harry chases Bellatrix and tries to use the Cruciatus Curse. Voldemort arrives and duels Dumbledore in the Atrium.
- Voldemort possesses Harry, but is driven out by Harry's grief and love for Sirius.
- **CHANGE:** Harry also feels love for Hermione in that moment, which helps drive Voldemort out. It's the clearest sign that his relationship is a strength, not a weakness, although Harry can't see that yet.
- **CHANGE:** Fudge sees Voldemort with his own eyes, but instead of admitting he's back, he starts covering it up. Ministry witnesses are quietly Obliviated. This sets up Fudge's alliance with Voldemort in Book 6.

## Part 8: Aftermath

- Dumbledore tells Harry about the prophecy: one of them must kill the other.
- Dumbledore admits he kept his distance from Harry to protect him, and it was a mistake.
- **NEW:** Dumbledore also restores Harry's prefect badge and explains why he chose Harry: he wanted Harry to know someone trusted him. Harry realizes the badge was a message he didn't hear.
- **CHANGE:** Harry doesn't learn from Dumbledore's mistake. He decides to make the same one with Hermione.
- Hermione recovers in the hospital wing.
- **NEW:** Harry visits her, but he can't say what he came to say. He sits with her until she falls asleep.
- Harry talks to Luna about grief and the voices behind the Veil, and starts to find peace.
- **CHANGE:** On the last night of the year, Harry breaks up with Hermione "to protect her." He believes the prophecy means everyone close to him will die.
- **CHANGE:** Hermione tells him it's a stupid reason. She isn't safer, she's just alone, and he doesn't get to decide what risks she takes. She doesn't fight him, because she can see he's grieving, but she makes it clear that she isn't going anywhere. This sets up Book 6.
- At King's Cross, the Order warns the Dursleys to treat Harry better.
- **NEW:** Hermione watches him leave with the Dursleys. Ron reminds her that Harry always comes back.

## Continuity checklist

- **Hermione's injury:** Her near-death at the Ministry gives Harry a concrete reason for the breakup, so it doesn't feel arbitrary.
- **Cho:** She's a background DA member only. Remove her scenes with Harry from the original, including the mistletoe kiss and the Valentine's date, which is cut entirely.
- **Ron's year:** He isn't a prefect and doesn't mind. His Quidditch struggles and Cup win are his own story, with no rivalry with Harry over the badge.
- **Prefect scenes:** Many existing scenes assume Ron and Hermione are the prefects, including meetings, rounds, and Ron's prefect privileges. Swap Harry in, or give those moments to Hermione alone after Harry loses his badge.
- **Harry's badge:** It's taken away mid-year and restored at the end of the book, so he's a prefect again in Book 6.
- **Élodie:** She appears only in letters, without a subplot.
- **Book 6 setups:** The Ministry reading owl post, the prophecy, and the breakup all feed into the surveillance-state plot and Harry and Hermione's slow way back to each other.

## Open decisions

1. **The breakup:** I'd have Harry end it on the last night, so it lands after everything else has fallen apart. The alternative is a quieter drift with no formal breakup, which is gentler but has less impact.
2. **How long they stay apart in Book 6:** I'd keep them apart for the first half, then bring them back together as the surveillance state forces them to rely on each other.
