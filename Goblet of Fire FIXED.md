# Goblet of Fire Reimagined: Outline

A planning outline for a fan-written alternate take on Book 4. Plot points are summarized from memory, so check details against the book as needed.

**Key:** Unmarked points stay as they are in the book. **CHANGE** marks a plot edit. **NEW** marks an added beat.

## Core changes

1. **Main romance:** Harry and Hermione are the book's central love story. Their friendship slowly becomes something more, and the Yule Ball is the turning point.
2. **Side addition:** Élodie Marchand, a half-Veela Beauxbatons student, gives Ron a light romantic subplot so he isn't left out.
3. **Knock-on effect:** Ron and Hermione's romantic tension is gone, along with the Krum jealousy subplot. Harry's crush on Cho fades, and Krum has no romantic interest in Hermione.

## Harry and Hermione's arc

The early changes are small. Most existing scenes stay the same, but Harry starts to notice things differently.

1. **Unnoticed (Parts 1-2):** They're simply best friends. Small moments show how well she knows him, but neither sees them as romantic.
2. **Trust (Part 3):** She believes him when nearly everyone else doubts him. This is the foundation of the romance.
3. **Awareness (Part 4):** Her fear and relief around the first task make Harry see how much she means to him, though he doesn't have words for it yet.
4. **Turning point (Part 5):** He asks her to the ball as a clumsy favor, then tries again honestly. On the night, they both realize it isn't only a favor.
5. **Stakes (Part 6):** Seeing her at the bottom of the lake removes any doubt. They become a couple after the second task, but keep it private.
6. **Pressure (Part 7):** Rita exposes the relationship before they're ready, testing whether they can handle the public attention.
7. **Partnership (Part 8):** She prepares him for the maze, and their relationship gives him more to lose.
8. **Strain (Part 9):** After the graveyard, she stays by him while he starts to shut down, setting up their conflict in Book 5.

## Élodie Marchand

Élodie stays in the background and appears only in a few short beats.

- **Age:** 14, the same year as Harry, Ron, and Hermione. Her relationship with Ron stays sweet and age-appropriate.
- **Personality:** Sharp, funny, and blunt.
- **Her arc:** She approaches Ron to get close to Harry Potter, finds that Harry isn't what she expected (he's shorter, for one thing), and ends up preferring Ron.

## Part 1: Summer and the World Cup

- Frank Bryce, the Riddle House caretaker, overhears Voldemort and Wormtail plotting and is killed. Bertha Jorkins has already been tortured for information and murdered.
- Harry wakes with his scar hurting and writes to Sirius.
- The Weasleys collect Harry from the Dursleys. Dudley eats a Ton-Tongue Toffee.
- Everyone travels by Portkey to the Quidditch World Cup, where Harry meets Cedric and Amos Diggory.
- Ireland beats Bulgaria even though Krum catches the Snitch. Fred and George win a bet with Ludo Bagman and are paid in leprechaun gold.
- Veela perform as Bulgaria's mascots, and Ron and Harry are visibly affected.
- **NEW:** During the panic of the riot, Hermione keeps hold of Harry's sleeve so they don't get separated. Neither of them thinks anything of it.
- Death Eaters riot, then flee when the Dark Mark appears. Winky is found holding Harry's stolen wand, and Crouch dismisses her.
- Hermione's outrage over Winky's treatment leads to S.P.E.W.

## Part 2: Back to Hogwarts

- Dumbledore cancels Quidditch and announces the Triwizard Tournament. Only students aged 17 or older can enter.
- Mad-Eye Moody arrives as the new Defense teacher. He turns Malfoy into a ferret, demonstrates the Unforgivable Curses, and teaches Harry to resist the Imperius Curse.
- Neville is shaken by the Cruciatus demonstration, and Moody gives him a Herbology book. This later matters for the gillyweed.
- Rita Skeeter's articles target the Weasleys and the Ministry.
- Hagrid's Blast-Ended Skrewts run through the year as a comic subplot.
- **NEW:** Hermione is the one who notices when Harry's scar is bothering him and pushes him to take it seriously. He's annoyed, but also a little glad that someone noticed.

## Part 3: The Goblet of Fire

- Beauxbatons and Durmstrang arrive, including Fleur, Krum, Madame Maxime, and Karkaroff.
- The Goblet chooses Fleur, Krum, Cedric, and then Harry as an unexpected fourth champion. The magical contract binds him to compete.
- Ron thinks Harry entered without telling him, and they fall out. Hermione stays by Harry's side. Most of the school turns against him, including "Potter Stinks" badges.
- **CHANGE:** Hermione's loyalty during the rift gets more space, including quiet scenes of the two of them alone for the first time. This becomes the emotional foundation of the romance.
- Rita interviews Harry for a fabricated profile during the wand weighing.
- **CHANGE:** Rita's profile invents a romance between Harry and Hermione. It's untrue at this point, and they're both mortified. Ron teases them, and for a day they're awkward around each other. It's the first time either of them has pictured the idea, and neither will admit it didn't sound as ridiculous as it should have.
- Sirius meets Harry in the common room fire and warns him about Karkaroff and Moody's enemies.

## Part 4: The First Task

- Hagrid shows Harry the dragons. Harry warns Cedric, and Moody nudges him to use his flying skills.
- Hermione drills him on the Summoning Charm.
- **CHANGE:** Hermione stays with him up to the task, adding to the emotional setup.
- Harry summons his Firebolt, beats the Hungarian Horntail, and takes the golden egg.
- Ron sees the danger Harry is in and they reconcile.
- **NEW:** Hermione's terrified relief after the task catches Harry off guard. He thinks about it later and can't work out why.
- The egg screams when opened, leaving Harry with a clue he can't decipher.

## Part 5: Christmas and the Yule Ball

- Hermione and the others visit the kitchens, where Dobby now works for wages and Winky is miserable.
- McGonagall announces the ball. Champions must open the dancing with partners.
- **CHANGE:** Harry never asks Cho. His crush can exist early on, but it fades as he realizes how he feels about Hermione.
- **CHANGE:** Harry asks Hermione badly, framing it as a favor, then tries again honestly. She accepts.
- **CHANGE:** In a small background moment, Krum asks Parvati to the ball and she happily accepts. Hermione isn't involved.
- **NEW:** Shortly before the ball, Élodie seeks out Ron to get an introduction to Harry. She's disappointed by the real Harry, who's shorter than she expected, but she enjoys arguing with Ron.
- Ron goes with Padma, who is unimpressed, and wears his awful old dress robes.
- **CHANGE:** Ron's jealous fight with Hermione at the end of the night is removed.

### The Yule Ball: Harry and Hermione's night

The ball's emotional question is simple: Harry asked Hermione as a friend, so why does tonight feel different?

1. **Waiting in the entrance hall:** Harry is nervous and has no idea what to expect. Then Hermione comes down the stairs in periwinkle robes with her hair done differently. For a moment, he doesn't recognize her. He says something clumsy, but she laughs, and he sees that she's nervous too.
2. **The champions' entrance:** McGonagall lines them up to walk in last. The whole school stares as they cross the hall, and Hermione quietly steadies him. It's the first time people have looked at him this year and he hasn't wanted to disappear.
3. **Dinner at the head table:** They sit with the other champions and the judges, and diners order by speaking to their golden plates.
   - After three years of easy conversation, they suddenly don't know what to say. Both are too polite, and it's awkward.
   - Hermione eyes the magical service and starts thinking about who's cooking it all. Her familiar S.P.E.W. mutter makes Harry laugh, and they relax back into being themselves.
   - Percy, seated beside them in Crouch's place, lectures Harry about Ministry business. Hermione rescues Harry with a well-timed question, and they share a look.
   - Harry realizes he hasn't thought about the egg, the Tournament, or his scar since they sat down. She's treating him like Harry rather than a champion, and he hadn't known how much he needed that.
4. **The opening dance:** The champions must dance first, and Harry is terrible at it. Hermione whispers instructions, and they end up laughing through it. By the end, he has stopped looking at his feet and is looking at her.
5. **The rest of the dancing:** The Weird Sisters take over, and the two of them stay together even when they could go back to Ron. They only notice how long they've been dancing when they're out of breath.
6. **Side moments:** Across the room, Élodie briefly asks Ron to dance after Padma leaves. Krum and Parvati are happily dancing in the background. These stay short so that the focus doesn't leave Harry and Hermione for long.
7. **The rose garden:** They step outside for air and walk among the fairy lights. It almost becomes a real conversation, but they're interrupted when they overhear Snape and Karkaroff arguing about the Dark Mark, then Hagrid telling Madame Maxime about his giant heritage. Rita is secretly listening too. They're both drawn back into worry, but now they're facing it together.
8. **The stairs at the end of the night:** Cedric stops Harry to tell him to take the egg to the prefects' bathroom. Afterward, Harry and Hermione sit on the steps with her shoes off. Neither wants to go upstairs. Their hands end up together, and neither mentions it or knows what to call it. It's the end of the favor and the start of what comes next.

## Part 6: Scandal and the Second Task

- Rita exposes Hagrid as half-giant. He hides in shame until Dumbledore, Harry, Ron, and Hermione coax him back.
- Harry solves the egg in the bath with Moaning Myrtle's help, and sees Crouch's name on the Marauder's Map in Snape's office. Moody borrows the map.
- **CHANGE:** With Hermione as the person Harry would miss most, she becomes his hostage. Ron stays out of the lake.
- **CHANGE:** Krum's hostage becomes a close Durmstrang friend. Cedric's stays Cho, and Fleur's stays Gabrielle.
- **NEW:** With Hermione taken the night before, Harry realizes how much he has relied on her. Ron stays up with him in the library, looking for a way to breathe underwater.
- Dobby brings Harry gillyweed.
- Harry stays to make sure all the hostages are saved, also rescuing Gabrielle. His moral fiber earns him the points to share first place with Cedric.
### The lake: Harry and Hermione

The egg's clue says the champions will lose what they'd miss most. Harry has guessed it's Hermione since she disappeared the night before, but seeing her underwater makes it real.

1. **Finding her:** At the merpeople's village, Harry finds the hostages tied to a statue, asleep and pale under the water. Hermione looks so still that for a moment he thinks the clue was literal and she's gone. It's the most frightened he has been all year, more than with the dragon.
2. **Cutting her free:** He frees her first, then refuses to leave until he knows everyone will be saved. He waits for the other champions, then frees Gabrielle when Fleur doesn't arrive. He knows staying might cost him the task, but he has Hermione beside him in the water, and he still won't leave anyone behind.
3. **Surfacing:** As his gillyweed wears off, he drags both girls upward. Hermione wakes the instant her head breaks the surface. The first thing she does is check on Gabrielle, and the second is ask why Harry took so long. He's too out of breath to answer and laughs instead.
4. **On the shore:** Madam Pomfrey wraps them in blankets and gives them a potion to stop the shivering. Ron is beside himself. Hermione works out what being taken means: she was the person he'd miss most, and the whole school just watched him prove it.
5. **The scores:** Harry is sure his delay has ruined his chances, but the judges reward his moral fiber, and he ties with Cedric for first. Fleur thanks him for Gabrielle. Hermione says he was an idiot for staying, but she's proud of him, which matters more to him than the points.
6. **The real conversation:** While everyone else is fussing over the scores, they finally have a moment alone at the edge of the lake. Harry tries to say what he felt when he saw her underwater, but he can't. Hermione admits she'd have been angry if it had been anyone else at the bottom of the lake for him. They agree that it isn't a favor anymore and that they're together.
7. **Keeping it quiet:** They decide not to tell anyone except Ron yet. It's new, Harry hates being stared at, and they want it to belong to them for a while. Ron is relieved they've finally worked it out and promises not to say anything, which he mostly manages.
8. **The beetle:** Neither of them notices a small beetle in Hermione's wet hair during the conversation. This is how Rita learns about the relationship, and it's the clue that leads Hermione to the truth later.

## Part 7: Rita's Attacks and the Crouch Mystery

- Harry, Ron, and Hermione meet Sirius in the cave near Hogsmeade and learn about Crouch's past, his son, and Bertha Jorkins.
- Rita publishes an article attacking Hermione. She receives hate mail, and Mrs. Weasley sends her a tiny Easter egg.

### Rita Skeeter: Harry and Hermione's first test

This is the first time the relationship faces the outside world. It comes a few weeks after the lake, while they're still keeping it private.

1. **The new angle:** Rita announces that they're together, which the school didn't know. Since she can't use Krum, she calls Hermione an ambitious Muggle-born who latched onto the famous boy and has been secretly doing his tasks for him. It attacks Hermione's character and Harry's right to be a champion at the same time.
2. **The detail that's wrong:** The article includes things Rita shouldn't know, like words from their private conversation after the lake. Hermione notices immediately. Harry is too angry to think about it yet.
3. **The public fallout:**
   - Snape reads the article aloud in Potions, and the Slytherins mutter about cheating.
   - Hermione receives hate mail, including a letter full of undiluted Bubotuber pus that burns her hands. Harry has to watch her go to the hospital wing because of him.
   - Mrs. Weasley believes the story and sends Hermione a tiny Easter egg, which hurts more than the hate mail.
4. **Their different reactions:**
   - **Harry:** He's furious and guilty. In his head, people get hurt for being close to him, and this is proof. It's an early version of what goes wrong between them in Book 5.
   - **Hermione:** The hate mail scares her, but being called a cheat makes her angry. She has spent her whole life proving she earns everything, so she refuses to hide.
5. **Their first fight:** Harry suggests that they keep their distance in public until things calm down. He thinks he's protecting her, but she hears him deciding for her, and maybe being embarrassed by her. Ron is stuck in the middle and handles it badly.
6. **Making up:** Harry realizes that hiding would let Rita win and would make Hermione carry the shame alone. He apologizes properly. The next morning, they walk into the Great Hall together holding hands. It's their first public statement as a couple, and also an act of defiance.
7. **Mrs. Weasley:** Harry writes to tell her the truth, including that he and Hermione really are together. Her reply is apologetic and a little too thrilled, and the next package includes a full-sized egg for Hermione. This adds warmth after the chapter's darkest stretch.
8. **Investigating together:** Hermione turns her anger into a project. Using the Marauder's Map and the Invisibility Cloak, they track where they were each time Rita learned something private. This makes them partners rather than just a couple and leads to her discovery in Part 8.
- Winky's grief and Crouch's strange absences keep building toward the mystery.
- Krum asks to talk with Harry privately near the forest. A delirious Crouch appears, then Krum is stunned and Crouch vanishes.
- **CHANGE:** Instead of asking about Hermione, Krum wants to ask Harry about Karkaroff's growing fear and what's really going on with the Tournament. This also ties the scene more closely to the mystery.
- In Divination, Harry has a vision of Voldemort punishing Wormtail, and his scar burns. He goes to Dumbledore's office.
- Harry falls into Dumbledore's Pensieve and sees Karkaroff name Death Eaters, Bagman tried and cleared, Snape vouched for, and Crouch sentencing his own son.
- Harry learns about Neville's parents, tortured into insanity by Death Eaters.

## Part 8: The Third Task and the Graveyard

- Harry trains with Ron and Hermione for the maze.
- **NEW:** The night before the task, Harry and Hermione get a quiet scene together. It's the calm before the graveyard, and they're happy.
- Mrs. Weasley and Bill come to watch.
- Hermione works out how Rita is getting her information.
- In the maze, Krum, under the Imperius Curse, attacks Cedric, and Harry stops him. Fleur is taken out as well. Harry passes a sphinx and fights off a giant spider with Cedric.
- Harry and Cedric agree to take the Cup together. It's a Portkey.
- In the graveyard, Wormtail kills Cedric on Voldemort's order. Harry is bound to Tom Riddle Sr.'s headstone.
- Wormtail's ritual uses the father's bone, his own hand, and Harry's blood to restore Voldemort's body.
- Voldemort summons the Death Eaters, reveals his plan, and duels Harry.
- Their wands connect in Priori Incantatem. The echoes of his victims, including Harry's parents, shield Harry long enough to escape with Cedric's body.

## Part 9: Aftermath

- Harry returns with Cedric's body. Moody takes him aside, then Dumbledore, Snape, and McGonagall stop him.
- Veritaserum reveals that Moody is Barty Crouch Jr., who has used Polyjuice all year and killed his father. The real Moody is rescued from a trunk.
- Harry tells Dumbledore what happened, with Sirius by his side. Fawkes heals him.
- Fudge brings a Dementor, which destroys Crouch Jr.'s soul before he can testify. Fudge refuses to believe Voldemort is back.
- Dumbledore sends Sirius and Snape on missions, and asks Mrs. Weasley to look after Harry.
- Harry gives his winnings to Fred and George for their joke shop, also resolving their bet with Bagman.
- Hermione catches Rita in her beetle Animagus form and keeps her in a jar.
- **CHANGE:** Harry is with Hermione when she catches Rita, as the result of the investigation they did together. Hermione sets Rita's terms: no writing for a year. Harry realizes she has quietly won the fight he wanted to protect her from. This also sets up Rita's honest interview with Harry in Book 5.
- Dumbledore honors Cedric at the leaving feast and urges unity.
- **CHANGE:** Harry and Hermione's final scene sets up the trouble ahead: he's starting to shut her out, even as she refuses to let him.
- **NEW:** Élodie leaves with Beauxbatons, and she and Ron agree to write over the summer.
- The Malfoy confrontation on the train and Harry's return to Privet Drive end the book.

## Continuity checklist

- **Ron's role:** He loses his place in the lake, so his friendship with Harry needs its own payoff, such as the library scene. Élodie is a bonus for him, not the replacement for that.
- **Book 5:** Élodie now goes home, which replaces the earlier idea of an exchange year and her conflict with Umbridge. She can stay a letter-writing side thread.
- **Ron and Hermione:** Their friendship still needs conflict and warmth, just not romantic tension.
- **Hermione's date:** Her transformation at the ball should still land, but it's now aimed at Harry rather than Krum.
- **Krum:** He stays honorable, with no romantic link to Hermione. His forest conversation with Harry is now about Karkaroff and the Tournament.
- **Fleur:** She can stay a separate character. Élodie might look up to her, or find her exhausting.
- **Rita:** She has more material to work with, which makes Hermione's capture of her more satisfying.
- **Crouch Jr.:** His plot isn't affected, but he should notice how Hermione and Ron's help could interfere with his plan.

## Locked decisions

1. **Krum's partner and hostage:** He takes Parvati to the ball, and a close Durmstrang friend is his hostage, since the task takes the person he'd miss most.
2. **When Harry and Hermione become official:** They become a couple privately after the lake, with only Ron told. Rita's article makes it public a few weeks later. The ball remains the turning point, leaving them room to grow.
